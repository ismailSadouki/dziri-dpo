Generate 20 candidate instruction-response examples for Algerian Darija in the category `code_switching`.

The goal is to create high-quality supervised fine-tuning data for an Algerian Darija language model that can understand and produce realistic Algerian code-switched language.

## Core language requirements

1. The user message must be naturally phrased in Algerian Darija.
2. The assistant response must naturally use Algerian Darija with realistic code-switching when appropriate.
3. The dataset MUST contain genuine variation in how Algerians mix languages.
4. Do NOT treat code-switching as simply inserting random French or English words.
5. Code-switching must be contextually motivated and linguistically natural.
6. Do not use Moroccan or Tunisian Darija.
7. Avoid excessive MSA vocabulary.
8. Arabic-script Darija should sound authentically Algerian.
9. Arabizi should resemble realistic Algerian online writing, not mechanical transliteration.

## What this category should teach

Focus on situations where Algerian speakers naturally alternate between:

* Algerian Darija + French
* Algerian Darija + English
* Darija + French + occasional English
* Arabic-script Darija + Latin-script technical/common terms
* Arabizi + French
* Arabizi + English

The objective is NOT to maximize the number of foreign words.

The objective is to reproduce realistic Algerian linguistic behavior.

## Important distinction

Do NOT generate sentences like:

"أنا عندي meeting pour le project parce que deadline قريب."

simply to demonstrate code-switching.

Instead, produce code-switching that an Algerian speaker could realistically use in conversation.

For example, a technical context might naturally contain:

"لازم ندير update للapplication قبل ما نعاود نجرب."

The foreign term should serve a communicative purpose.
