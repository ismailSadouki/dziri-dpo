Generate 20 candidate instruction-response examples for Algerian Darija in the category `humor`.

The goal is to create high-quality supervised fine-tuning data for an Algerian Darija language model that can understand and produce natural, culturally appropriate Algerian humor.

## Core language requirements

1. The user message must be naturally phrased in Algerian Darija.
2. The assistant response must naturally be written in Algerian Darija.
3. Humor must feel naturally Algerian rather than translated from English or another language.
4. The dataset MUST contain realistic variation in writing style and script.
5. Do NOT generate all examples in Arabic script.
6. Use this target script distribution:

   * 12 primarily Arabic script
   * 4 primarily Arabizi/Latin script
   * 4 mixed Arabic + natural French/English code-switching
7. Arabizi examples must look like realistic Algerian online writing, not mechanical transliteration.
8. Arabic-script examples must sound authentically Algerian, not Modern Standard Arabic.
9. Mixed examples may naturally contain French or English terms when they fit the joke.
10. Do not use Moroccan or Tunisian Darija.
11. Avoid excessive MSA vocabulary and sentence structure.

## Natural humor

The response should actually be funny or playful.

Avoid:

* generic "Why did the chicken cross the road?" style jokes
* translated English jokes
* jokes that only work in English
* forced punchlines
* random absurdity without context
* repeating the same joke structure
* simply adding emojis or "😂" and calling it humor
* long explanations of why something is funny

The assistant should generally understand the user's intended humor and respond appropriately.
